Frame Studio Originals — bundled music library
==============================================

All tracks in this folder were synthesized from scratch for Frame Studio by
the project's own `scripts/generate-music.py` generator. They contain no
samples, no third-party recordings, and no downloaded material.

License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

You may copy, modify, distribute and use these tracks for any purpose,
including commercial projects, without attribution. Attribution is
appreciated but not required:

    "Night Drive", "Sunrise", "Deep Focus", "Action Cuts", "Soft Keys",
    "Street Pulse", "Bright Horizon", "Hollow Hall", "Neon Arcade"
    — Frame Studio Originals (CC0 1.0)

The app never streams these files from the internet. They are bundled with
the build and copied into the user's local project only when chosen.
